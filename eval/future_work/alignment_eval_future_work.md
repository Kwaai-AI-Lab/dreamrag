# Alignment and Safety Evaluation — Future Work

## Purpose

This document outlines future evaluation directions for assessing behavioral reliability, safety, and alignment characteristics in Dream RAG outputs.

This evaluation category is outside the current retrieval-quality and graph-refinement scope.

## Motivation

Retrieval quality does not automatically imply safe or aligned behavior. Future evaluation may examine whether graph refinement affects harmfulness, refusal behavior, or safety compliance.

## Candidate Evaluation Dimensions

- Harmfulness
- Toxicity
- Refusal Quality
- Safety Compliance
- Constitutional Adherence

## Potential Research Questions

- Does improved retrieval grounding reduce unsafe or unsupported outputs?
- Do dream cycles affect refusal consistency?
- Can graph refinement improve safety-relevant behavior?

## Deferred Scope

This work is intentionally deferred because safety and alignment evaluation introduces a distinct research question beyond retrieval quality.
