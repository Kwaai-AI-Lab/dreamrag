# Trustworthiness Evaluation — Future Work

## Purpose

This document outlines future evaluation directions for assessing the trustworthiness of Dream RAG outputs.

This evaluation category is outside the current retrieval-quality and graph-refinement scope.

## Motivation

Retrieval quality measures whether relevant information is found. Trustworthiness evaluation asks whether retrieved and generated outputs are reliable, consistent, and appropriately supported.

## Candidate Evaluation Dimensions

- Groundedness
- Provenance
- Consistency
- Confidence Calibration
- Composite Trust Score

## Potential Research Questions

- Do dream cycles improve output reliability over time?
- Does graph refinement improve provenance traceability?
- Are confidence scores calibrated against retrieval quality?

## Deferred Scope

This work is intentionally deferred because it introduces a distinct research question beyond retrieval quality.
